patient_id:
Unique identifier for each patient.

encounter_id:
Unique identifier for each clinical encounter (visit).

visit_start:
Datetime when encounter begins.

visit_stop:
Datetime when encounter ends.

encounter_type:
Text description of type of clinical visit.

lab_value:
Observed laboratory or vital sign value (character, may require numeric conversion).

lab_units:
Units corresponding to lab_value.

med_code:
Medication code.

med_desc:
Medication description.
